# Asset provenance

The final app icon is generated using the built-in ImageGen tool, edited into a vintage hand-drawn sketch at the user's explicit request. The rejected photorealistic version is not used by the app.

Final master: `dist/assets/icon-sketch-master.png`. Derived outputs: `icon-v2-512.png`, `icon-v2-192.png`, `apple-touch-icon-v2.png`, `favicon-v2.png`, `icon-maskable-v2.png`. Raster resizing only was performed with FFmpeg; the generated composition was not changed. The 512px maskable version adds a safe inset.

## Final image prompt

Edit this app icon into a beautiful VINTAGE HAND-DRAWN SKETCH, not photorealistic. Keep its main idea: one instantly recognizable vintage camcorder with an S-shaped structural body, a lens on the right, small flip-out screen on the left and carry handle. Redraw the entire image as an elegant 1980s industrial-design sketch / old camera instruction-manual engraving: confident irregular fine ink contours, hand-drawn pencil strokes and sparse delicate cross-hatching, warm ivory and beige ink on a very dark muted olive green background with subtle aged paper grain. The lens and screen should also be drawn, with simple olive and ochre shapes, no photographic scene in the LCD. Flatten the volumes into a sophisticated illustration, a little perspective retained, but absolutely NO metallic 3D rendering, no glossy realistic surfaces, no cinematic realism, no photo textures. Make the S silhouette a natural large part of the sketch rather than printed lettering. Improve proportions so it feels like a charming recognizable small camcorder, not a giant letter sculpture. Center the single illustrated camera at about 68% canvas width with generous breathing room; strong legibility at 180px iPhone app-icon size. Limited vintage palette: deep moss/olive, faded beige, warm cream and a tiny muted burnt-ochre record mark. Opaque square full-bleed olive background, no outer rounded tile, no separate border, no words, no added typography or branding, no collage, no presentation mockup. The result must be a premium vintage illustrated sketch icon.

Other raster provenance: `scene.jpg` is the Unsplash lake asset documented in README; `demo.mp4` is a pan-and-zoom derivative, not camcorder footage. Camera shelf illustrations are five code-native SVG drawings in `camera-art.js`. Grain and tape-strand background assets are authored SVGs. Fonts are locally bundled Alexandria (OFL) and Bodoni Moda (OFL); corresponding licenses are in assets. Mediabunny bundle is unmodified MPL-2.0.
